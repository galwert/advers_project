\begin{abstract}

Adversarial attacks transfer between LLMs due to shared representation geometry. We leverage this by training a LoRA adapter that minimizes Centered Kernel Alignment (CKA) between a defender and an anchor model on harmful prompts. Across seven models spanning five architecture families (7B--14B scale), our defense reduces cross-model attack success rate to 0--5\% against transferred GCG suffixes while preserving benign utility. Existing defenses, evaluated exclusively against same-model attacks, leave residual cross-model vulnerabilities that our approach addresses for the first time.

\end{abstract}
